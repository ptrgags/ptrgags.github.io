## PICO-8: Hitomezashi Editor

See [Lexaloffle BBS link](https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?tid=158802)

Lately I've been exploring the PICO-8 fantasy game engine as another environment 
for creative coding. I've made several cartridges in my 
[`pico8-carts`](https://github.com/ptrgags/pico8-carts) repository, but this one 
is the first I've posted to the Lexaloffle bulletin board service (BBS).

This cartridge lets you explore Hitomezashi embroidery patterns. See the post
linked above for more details.

## Desmos Graph: Involute Gear

See [Desmos Graph](https://www.desmos.com/calculator/zim3g3v74v)

I've been exploring the math of gears lately. A friend of mine who is into 
horology (study of timekeeping) had shown me a video series about the geometry 
of gears. In time, I want to make animations involving gear trains. As a first
step, I made this graph to determine how to render an involute gear. 

## WIP Visualization: Yamaha DX7 Synthesizer Patch Visualizer

See [DX7 Patch Visualizers](https://ptrgags.dev/p5-sketchbook/DX7PatchViewer/) in my `p5-sketchbook` repo.

I have an interest in music synthesizers. Beyond making cool sounds, I'm interested in the math behind them. 
Lately I've been digging deeper into frequency modulation (FM) synthesis. To help with this endeavor, I'm making myself tools
to work with patches from the Yamaha DX7 (a classic synth from the 1980s) as 
realized in the emulator [Dexed](https://asb2m10.github.io/dexed/) and the 
[M-VAVE FM1](https://www.m-vave.com/product?id=fm-1) synthesizer.

This page takes a cartridge file from these synths, and lets you view the
parameters with more intuitive visuals. Or at least that's the end goal, it's
still a work in progress.
