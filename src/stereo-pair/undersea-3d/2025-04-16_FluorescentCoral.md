<p>
    From witnessing this tank at the aquarium, I learned that corals are 
    fluorescent (i.e. they glow under UV light). Later, I learned from 
    <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QotFxwrlD0A&list=PLXPyqwUeMof531MhQ8zK4tDEhG9xryXVo&index=5&t=5s&ab_channel=PARLEYCHANNEL">this video</a>
    by Coral Morphologic and Parley <em>why</em> corals fluoresce. It's a
    defense against harsh UV light. The coral only absorbs part of the
    light energy, and emits the rest as light of a different color. In
    this case, the fluorescence is the green and orange glow from each 
    polyp.
</p>
